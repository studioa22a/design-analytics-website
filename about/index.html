About us

Good people to have on the job.

We are a small team of registered architects in Melbourne, working on residential estates and commercial buildings alongside developers, project managers, property owners and agents. We've sat where you sit — waiting on an approval, chasing a consultant, explaining a delay to a buyer or a tenant — and we built Design Analytics so you don't have to.

Working with us

You'll know who to call, and they'll pick up.

One person, start to finish

You deal with the architect doing the work, not an account manager. They know your estate or your building, and the people working on it, by name.

A date, and we keep it

We tell you when you'll have an answer before we start, and we tell you early if something is going to move it. No chasing.

Plain answers

Yes, no, or here's what needs to change — in language a builder, a tenant, a buyer or a board can act on. We'll say what we think, and why.

We leave things tidy

When the job's done you have everything in one place, and it still makes sense a year later when someone else picks it up.

Who we work alongside

Whoever is carrying the project that week.

Developers

You set the standard for an estate or a building. We hold it for you — lot after lot, tenancy after tenancy — so it turns out the way you intended.

Project managers

You need the design piece to land on the date in the programme. We give you one point of contact and a status you can read without asking.

Property owners

Land coming to market or a building to keep leased. We size the work to the job, keep you across cost and timing, and leave the records with the property.

Sales and leasing agents

You're the one the buyer or tenant rings. We make sure you have the answer — what they can build or fit out, how long it takes, what it will look like.

Where we stand

Registered, insured, and in it for the long run.

Our work spans both sides of development. On commercial buildings in Melbourne we design upgrades and tenant fit-outs. On residential estates we write design guidelines nationally and assess every lot across Victoria.

Every member of the team who makes a decision on your project is a registered architect.

We'd rather do a smaller number of things well, for clients who come back, than be everywhere at once.

Bring the estate, the building or the problem. We'll tell you plainly whether we're the right people for it.
